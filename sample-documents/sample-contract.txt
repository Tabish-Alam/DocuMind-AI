SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2026, between
Northwind Consulting LLC ("Provider") and Bramble Retail Group Inc. ("Client").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Provider agrees to deliver a customer analytics dashboard, including data pipeline
integration, a web-based reporting interface, and monthly performance reviews.
Implementation will occur in three phases: Discovery (2 weeks), Build (6 weeks),
and Rollout (2 weeks), for a total estimated timeline of 10 weeks from the
Effective Date.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
The total contract value is $84,500 USD, payable in three installments:
$28,000 upon signing, $34,000 upon completion of the Build phase, and $22,500
upon final acceptance. Invoices are due within 30 days of receipt. Late payments
accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Services
are delivered and accepted, unless terminated earlier. Either party may terminate
with 30 days' written notice. Client may terminate for cause immediately if
Provider fails to cure a material breach within 15 days of written notice.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to keep confidential all proprietary information disclosed
during the engagement for a period of 3 years following termination.

Client Sponsor: David Okafor, Director of Operations, Bramble Retail Group Inc.
